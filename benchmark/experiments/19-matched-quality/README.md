# 19 matched quality

2026-09-13. experiment 11 calibrated nineteen encoders to one ssimulacra2 and lost the script that did it. this is that step as a tool in the repo, run on the four recipes the forge ships, and it changes what one of the numbers in production means.

hypothesis: at the same mean ssimulacra2 the ranking of experiment 11 holds for the forge's own recipes: avif from libaom is 12 to 13% smaller than the svt-av1 all-intra stream with the still tune, and the still tune is worth about 10% of bytes against svt's default tune at equal quality.
method: benchmark/tools/crf_for_target.py --target 61.96, the calibration point of experiment 11; for each of av1 still speed 6, av1 default tune speed 6, avif speed 6 and avif speed 8, bisect the quality knob until two adjacent knobs bracket the target on frames-96 (mean ssimulacra2 of the decoded frame against the letterboxed source png), then encode the whole sample-2048 at both brackets and interpolate the bytes at the target; the production avif recipe (q48 speed 8) measured once more on the same sample; the forge's encoders and letterbox throughout, apple m4, svt-av1 4.2.0, aom 3.15.0.
verdict: half refuted. the still tune holds and then some: svt's default tune needs 21.6% more bytes for the same fidelity (82.4 MB against 67.8 MB on the 2048 cards). avif does not hold as measured: at matched quality libaom speed 6 is 5.0% smaller than the av1 still stream, not 12.4%, and libaom speed 8, the recipe urna #137 made the stills profile, is 1.1% larger. the 13% on the full corpus compared an avif at ssimulacra2 58.6 with an av1 stream at 63.2; the two files were 4.6 points apart on the ruler that was supposed to match them. experiment 11's own baseline was the tune-4 fallback its battery found, 70.09 MB at 61.96; the fixed forge encodes tune 3, byte-identical to the battery's tune-3 row two weeks later, and sits at 63.22 for 71.05 MB.

i wanted this tool for a boring reason, to stop calibrating by hand, and the first run of it took apart a number i had repeated in three documents. the avif profile went into urna yesterday on the strength of 13%. the 13% is real as bytes: 1.196 GB against 1.374 GB on the full corpus. it is not real as a comparison, because the avif candidate was encoded at q48 with speed 8 and lands at 58.6 on the 96 frames, while the av1 stream it was measured against lands at 63.2. put both on 61.96 and speed 8 needs 68.5 MB where the stream needs 67.8. speed 6 gets you 5%, at twice the encode time and with the per-image decode path that the embed stage pays for.

how the number got there is worth writing down, because it is two honest measurements added wrong. experiment 11 calibrated avif at speed 6 (q48, 61.4 MB, 60.72 on the ruler, inside the +-2 tolerance) against a baseline that was svt with the tune silently fallen back to 4 (70.09 MB at 61.96). the battery found that fallback and the forge was fixed, so every av1 file since encodes tune 3 and is 1.4% larger at 1.3 points more quality. then the profile was written with speed 8, not 6, because speed 8 is what avifenc does by default and what the candidate had actually been built with. each step is documented. the sum is a profile that is slower to embed, no smaller at equal quality, and labeled as 13% smaller.

two smaller things fell out. aom 3.14.1 to 3.15.0 moves the same knob by 1.5% of bytes and half a point of ssimulacra2, which is the cross-version half of experiment 18's caveat, now with a number on it. and svt-av1 4.2.0 reproduced the tune-3 row of the battery byte for byte across two weeks and a different script, which is what the toolchain record in the manifest is for.

what i would do with the stills profile is not mine to decide, so it is a urna issue with this table: keep avif at speed 6 and say 5%, or go back to the stream. the tool is the part that stays.

provenance: raw json under `data/`; the 2026-08-31 rows are read from experiment 11's `battery.json`. encode seconds were taken while the five-model build held the gpu and are not a speed measurement.
